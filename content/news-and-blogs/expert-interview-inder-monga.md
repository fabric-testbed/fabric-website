*In the below article originally published on the [Berkeley Lab News Center](https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2025/04/07/expert-interview-inder-monga/), FABRIC leadership team member Inder Monga, Executive Director of Energy Sciences Network (ESnet), discusses how ESNet supports the nation's scientists.*

Scientific breakthroughs rely on seamless data movement, much like a circulatory system delivering vital knowledge. Energy Sciences Network (ESnet), the Department of Energy's sole high-performance networking user facility, keeps this system running and enables breakthroughs that shape our world.

The podcast features [**Inder Monga**](https://profiles.lbl.gov/24934-inder-monga/about), the Director of the Scientific Networking Division at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (Berkeley Lab) and Executive Director of ESnet. In addition to managing ESnet, Inder works to advance the science of networking for collaborative and distributed research applications. He is the principal investigator for the Quantum Application Network Testbed for Novel Entanglement Technology (QUANT-NET) project and the co-PI of the National Science Foundation's FABRIC testbed project.
