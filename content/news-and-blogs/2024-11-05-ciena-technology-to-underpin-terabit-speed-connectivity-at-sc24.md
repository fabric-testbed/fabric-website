_This article was originally published on_ _[Ciena](https://www.ciena.com/about/newsroom/press-releases/ciena-technology-to-underpin-terabit-speed-connectivity-for-scinet-network-at-sc24)_.

[Ciena](https://www.ciena.com/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) announced that its [optical](https://www.ciena.com/products#optical-networking/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) and [routing and switching](https://www.ciena.com/products#routing-and-switching/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) innovations will once again support the [SCinet](https://sc24.supercomputing.org/scinet/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) network at [SC24](https://sc24.supercomputing.org/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease), taking place November 17–22 at Atlanta’s [Georgia World Congress Center](https://www.gwcca.org/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) (GWCC). Constructed specifically for the annual SC conference by a team of industry, academia, and government experts, SCinet is a testbed and demonstration network that will provide a massive 8.71Tb/s of bandwidth from the GWCC to R&E networks across the globe, allowing the SC community to engage in data-intensive research, experiments, and demonstrations. Additionally, Ciena will feature demos of its scalable and sustainable solutions for scientific research and discovery networks in Booth #1940.

**Ciena’s SC24 Highlights:**

-   **SCinet Connectivity:** Ciena will power 400GE circuits between Chicago’s [StarLight International/National Communications Exchange Facility](https://www.startap.net/starlight/ABOUT/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) and the GWCC using [WaveLogic 5 Extreme](https://www.ciena.com/products/wavelogic/wavelogic-5/extreme/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) modems over [Internet2](https://internet2.edu/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) spectrum. Ciena will also provide 400GE circuits between the GWCC and the Joint Big Data Testbed in McLean, Virginia, using the [8190](https://www.ciena.com/products/8190/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) coherent aggregation router and [WaveLogic 5 Nano](https://www.ciena.com/products/wavelogic/wavelogic-5/nano/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) coherent pluggable transceivers over [ESnet](https://www.es.net/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) spectrum (1,300km route). In addition, Ciena’s [6500 Reconfigurable Line System](https://www.ciena.com/products/6500-reconfigurable-line-system/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) (RLS) will extend the Internet2 photonic backbone into the GWCC, while its [8110](https://www.ciena.com/products/8110/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) coherent aggregation router will extend the [NSF](https://new.nsf.gov/about/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease)\-funded [FABRIC](https://fabric-testbed.org/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) testbed to the show floor.
-   **Ciena Booth #1940:** Ciena will highlight its latest innovations, including [WaveLogic 6 Extreme](https://www.ciena.com/products/wavelogic/wavelogic-6/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) and [WaveLogic 6 Nano](https://www.ciena.com/products/wavelogic/wavelogic-6/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease) coherent pluggable optics. The Company will perform collaborative research demonstrations with the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/en/?utm_source=PR&utm_medium=PressRelease), showcasing federated learning (FL) and microservice applications for AI-driven engine failure detection in the aviation industry, as well as in-network machine learning.
-   **Traveling FABRIC Node (tfNode):** Ciena’s mobile tfNode will be featured as part of the SCinet network and hosted in booth #1940. The tfNode provides high-speed connectivity, capable of multiple x400Gb/s, making it possible for researchers and experimenters to conduct large-scale projects in fields such as distributed computing, storage, VR, AI, and machine learning.
