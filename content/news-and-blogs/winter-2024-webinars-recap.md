The FABRIC team hosted three webinars in Winter 2024 for its user base across two series.

## Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users

### ERN CryoEm and FABRIC — November 26, 2024

Maureen Dougherty, ERN — Through the lens of the ERN CryoEM project, Maureen showcases why FABRIC was selected and how its facility ports are innovatively used to support complex scientific needs.

### When Federated Learning Meets FABRIC — December 17, 2024

Zilinghan Li, Argonne National Laboratory — This webinar offers a deep dive into the APPFL experiment on FABRIC, covering the rationale behind selecting FABRIC as the experimental platform.

## Mastering FABRIC: Tips and Tricks

### Facility Ports — November 25, 2024

Tom Lehman, FABRIC Team | Ben Formby, Clemson University — This webinar explores the powerful capabilities of FABRIC's facility ports, including practical guidance on how they can be utilized effectively within experimental setups.
