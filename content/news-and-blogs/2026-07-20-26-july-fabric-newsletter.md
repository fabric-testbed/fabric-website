## FABRIC News

### LoomAI Hub Is Now Available at loomai.net

_The new recommended way to use FABRIC_

LoomAI Hub is live at [loomai.net](https://loomai.fabric-testbed.net), and it is now the recommended way to use FABRIC. Sign in with your FABRIC identity to launch your own LoomAI environment right in your browser, with no local setup required.

LoomAI is our open-source, browser-based interface that replaces command-line and Jupyter notebook workflows with a visual, point-and-click experience. It brings drag-and-drop topology building, integrated management tools, an AI assistant for experiment design and troubleshooting, reusable Weaves, and the Artifact Marketplace together in one place. All AI features run on models hosted on FABRIC, so your work stays in a secure, research-focused environment.

If you have been working through the portal or Jupyter notebooks, now is a great time to move to LoomAI Hub. Go to [loomai.net](https://loomai.fabric-testbed.net) and sign in to get started.

Want more detail or have questions? The team has posted a full announcement in the new LoomAI forum on the FABRIC forum, where you can read more and connect with other users.

### Educational Use: Using FABRIC for Class Projects

As a national research infrastructure for cutting-edge and exploratory research in networking, distributed computing, and science applications, FABRIC encourages faculty to use the infrastructure for teaching their classes. Training next-generation researchers to imagine and construct new computing and networking experiments at scale is an important goal for FABRIC. FABRIC has created sample experiments and Jupyter notebooks that are ready to be used by networking and systems classes for instructors.

These experiments include basic ping, network routing, exploring IPv6, TCP analysis, traffic generation, setting up a web server, using Ansible to manage a set of nodes, etc. They are available via GitHub as well as the FABRIC Artifact Manager. Of course, you can create your own assignments for students to do on the FABRIC infrastructure. The FABRIC team provides support from creating an education project, enrolling students, to answering questions from instructors and students. [More information can be found here.](https://learn.fabric-testbed.net/knowledge-base/fabric-education-materials/)
