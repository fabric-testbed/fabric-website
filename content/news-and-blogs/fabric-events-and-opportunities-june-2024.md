## Registration Open: KNIT 9

September 24-27, 2024 | Kansas City, MO — KNIT 9, the next FABRIC Community Workshop, will include small-group, hands-on FABRIC tutorial sessions and advanced training topics. Registration is available on the [MERIF website](https://merif.renci.org/).
