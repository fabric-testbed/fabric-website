The FABRIC team hosted three webinars in Q3 and two webinars in Q4 2025 for its user base across two series. Each series is aimed at FABRIC users at different stages in their FABRIC journey.

# Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users

## Low Latency 5G Core Experiments on FABRIC — July 15, 2025

Presenters: K.K. Ramakrishnan (UC Riverside), Shixiong Qi (University of Kentucky), Jyh-Cheng Chen (National Chiao Tung University)

The webinar offered an overview of L25GC+, a high-performance 5G core built on OpenNetVM, showcasing how its shared-memory architecture dramatically reduced control-plane latency and improved overall performance.

## Programmable Data Plane — September 16, 2025

Presenter: Dagim Mindaye, Alfred University

The webinar offered a look at how programmable data planes can extend Kubernetes, with the presenter showcasing work on implementing a Kubernetes load balancer and exploring the QUIC protocol for data center environments.

## Bluefield NICs — November 11, 2025

Presenter: Elie Kfoury, University of South Carolina

The webinar offered a deep dive into BlueField NIC experimentation on FABRIC, demonstrating how Data Processing Units (DPUs) can offload workloads, accelerate data processing, and open new possibilities for distributed computing and networking research.

# Mastering FABRIC: Tips and Tricks

## Exploring FabFed for Cloud Integration — August 19, 2025

Presenter: Xi Yang, ESNet

This session offered a comprehensive look at how FabFed is transforming multi-platform experimentation by enabling seamless resource instantiation and management across FABRIC, CloudLab, and Chameleon.

## GPU Nodes on Federated Testbeds Workflow — October 21, 2025

Presenter: Benjamin Formby, Clemson

This session offered a look at how FABRIC enabled testbed administrators and AI researchers to connect and scale GPU resources across multiple platforms.
