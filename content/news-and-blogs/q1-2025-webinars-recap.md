The FABRIC team hosted three webinars in Q1 2025 for its user base across two series. Each series is aimed at FABRIC users at different stages in their FABRIC journey.

## Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users

### EJFAT and FABRIC — January 21, 2025

Ilya Baldin, Jefferson Lab — The webinar features a tutorial providing an overview of how FABRIC can support ambitious experiments, including a showcase of the groundbreaking EJFAT experiment.

### Federated Digital Pathology with FABRIC — March 18, 2025

Cody Bumgardner, Vaiden Logan, and Mitchell Klusty; University of Kentucky — This webinar explores how FABRIC empowers researchers in federated digital pathology, providing a flexible and scalable platform for experiments.

## Mastering FABRIC: Tips and Tricks

### Routed Networks with Performance Enhancements via Hardware Offload — February 18, 2025

Justas Balcas, ESNet — This session offers a comprehensive look at how cutting-edge advancements are shaping the future of network performance, utilizing FRR routers and hardware offload to achieve significant performance gains.
