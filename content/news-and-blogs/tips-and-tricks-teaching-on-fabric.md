*Getting started with using FABRIC in classrooms*

FABRIC can be used in a wide range of classes covering topics such as computer networking, operating systems, distributed systems, security, cloud computing, web services, content distribution systems, edge computing, and federated learning. FABRIC provides a unique environment where students can control every aspect of the system from the topology of the network, to the configuration of the virtual machine hardware, to the operating systems and software stacks run on the VMs.

For new instructors planning to onboard students and incorporate FABRIC in their curriculum, the FABRIC team has put together a reference guide with information on creating a class project, creating student accounts, available resources, example assignments, and other step-by-step instructions. For more detailed information, please refer to the article [FABRIC Information for Instructors](https://learn.fabric-testbed.net/knowledge-base/fabric-information-for-instructors/).

## Resources Available to Instructors

The FABRIC team has deployed a dedicated FABRIC rack to support educational use, called the EDUKY rack. Class experiments using the EDUKY rack share many similarities with research experiments, including using JupyterHub, leveraging shared programmable NICs, creating L2 and L3 networks, and utilizing the FABlib and MFlib services.

## Instructional Materials Available to Instructors

A wide range of instructional material is available including assignments, projects, and tutorials. The set of GENI materials have been converted to [FABRIC teaching materials](https://github.com/fabric-testbed/teaching-materials) and/or [Multi-Platform Teaching Materials](https://teaching-on-testbeds.github.io/).

## How to Get Started

Instructors should submit a [Class Request Form](https://fabric-testbed.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/2/group/11/create/64) via the FABRIC ticket system and provide detailed information about course assignments and resources needed.

## Getting Help as an Instructor

Instructors are encouraged to subscribe to the [FABRIC Educators Forum](https://learn.fabric-testbed.net/forums/forum/fabric-educators/) where they can raise questions about teaching their course on FABRIC.
