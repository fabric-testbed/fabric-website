*FABRIC testbed used to simulate local 5G hotspots set up across transcontinental distances*

Professor Akihiro Nakao at the University of Tokyo and Fatou Secka are working to extend local 5G secure networking across transcontinental network slices. Their goal is to develop a solution providing secure, high-performance connectivity for global businesses and industrial IoT applications.

The researchers developed a way to extend secure local 5G slicing to transcontinental networks by connecting the local 5G network to a dynamic core configuration with SIM-based authentication and integrated network infrastructure.

Using FABRIC, the researchers established simulated local 5G hotspots in Tokyo, Japan, and Dallas, Texas, interconnected via a trans-oceanic link. They demonstrated an Inter-site Round-Trip Time (RTT) averaging around 145 ms, fast enough for most business and IoT applications.

"FABRIC's distributed cloud resources allowed us to realistically assess the performance and scalability of our dynamic core configuration," said Secka.
