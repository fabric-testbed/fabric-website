Please be advised that FABRIC will undergo a multi-day maintenance period during the week of **July 28-Aug 1, 2025**. This maintenance is essential for the continued stability and performance of the testbed.

This work will be disruptive across all FABRIC sites. To facilitate this maintenance, **all active slices will need to be deleted prior to the maintenance window.** Please note that slices will not be recovered after the maintenance is complete. Therefore, it is crucial that you **back up all necessary data** from your slivers.

Your SSH keys, data stored on persistent volumes, and experiment notebooks will remain unaffected by this maintenance.

We understand that this is an interruption to your research and experimentation, and we apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.

The FABRIC Team
