Over the summer, the FABRIC team launched three new webinar series for its user base.

## FABRIC 101

### Hello FABRIC — July 15, 2024

Komal Thareja, FABRIC Team — Overview of how FABRIC can serve as the ideal testbed for research projects, with a live demo on creating an account and starting your first experiment.

## Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users

### Debuggable, Programmable Networks — July 16, 2024

Nik Sultana, Illinois Institute of Technology — Overview of a recent P4 experiment, sharing why he chose FABRIC and how straightforward the process was.

### SciStream and FABRIC — August 19, 2024

Joaquin Chung, Argonne National Laboratory — Introduction to SciStream and its integration with FABRIC.

## Mastering FABRIC: Tips and Tricks

### Introduction to Tailscale — June 17, 2024

Paul Ruth, FABRIC Team — Introduction and demonstration of Tailscale for simplifying secure network connectivity to FABRIC experiments.

### Exploring Different Network Interfaces — August 20, 2024

Paul Ruth, FABRIC Team — Comprehensive overview of FABRIC's different types of network interfaces and their capabilities.
