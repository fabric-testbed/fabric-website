The FABRIC team hosted four webinars in Autumn 2024 for its user base across two series.

## Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users

### AI/ML Experiments — September 17, 2024

Ben Formby, Clemson University — Ben delved into AI/ML experiments leveraging FABRIC's capabilities, providing a comprehensive overview of his experiment involving medical images.

### Poseidon and FABRIC — October 21, 2024

Cong Wang (RENCI), Mariam Kiran (ORNL), Imtiaz Mahud (Berkeley Lab) — Introduction and demonstration of Poseidon, a tool designed to optimize big data flows across various facilities.

## Mastering FABRIC: Tips and Tricks

### Artifact Manager — September 16, 2024

Komal Thareja and Michael J. Stealey, FABRIC Team — Overview of the Artifact Manager for packaging and sharing complete, repeatable FABRIC experiments.

### How to Use the Data in FABRIC — October 22, 2024

Jim Griffioen, FABRIC Team | Charles Carpenter, University of Kentucky — A deep dive into utilizing the wealth of data measured on the FABRIC platform.
