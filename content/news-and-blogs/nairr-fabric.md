The National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) is a vision for a shared national research infrastructure for responsible discovery and innovation in AI.

The NAIRR pilot brings together computational, data, software, model, training and user support resources. Led by the U.S. NSF in partnership with 10 other federal agencies and 25 non-governmental partners.

FABRIC is a distributed network and computing facility with equipment at 35 sites that offers the following capabilities for AI experimenters:

- Robust in-network processing capabilities, including FPGAs
- Ability to simulate edge use cases for inferencing or federated learning
- Distributed computing and storage for distributed AI paradigms
- Flexible Jupyter notebooks
- Precision timing for packet level time stamping
- Ideal facilities for reinforcement learning for autonomous cyber security
- Quality of Service (QoS) capabilities between sites
- Large capacity data plane with instrumentation
- Connectivity to HPC, Cloud and campuses

To learn more about NAIRR, check out the [NAIRR website](https://nairrpilot.org/) and [NSF program page](https://new.nsf.gov/focus-areas/artificial-intelligence/nairr).
