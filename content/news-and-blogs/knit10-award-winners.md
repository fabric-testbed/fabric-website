Congratulations to the KNIT10 Award Winners Kriti Goel, Saurya Saha, Vaneshi Ramdhony, and Hyunsuk Bang! Awardees were selected for their innovative uses of FABRIC testbed resources, experiment design, and execution.

## Best Experiment

### Expedited Internet Bypass Protocol

*Kriti Goel*

This project addresses the growing complexity in Internet routing by simplifying the Internet Protocol and its routing protocols. The researchers' solution protocol is scalable, has inherent fast failure detection and is more stable. The team has a scalable routing technique that can replace OSPF and BGP, providing significant reduction in router operations, instability and cost while improving performance.

## Best Classroom Use

### Optimizing CDN Architectures: Multi-Metric Algorithmic Breakthroughs for Edge and Distributed Performance

*Saurya Saha*

The paper focuses on enhancing Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) by deploying dynamic algorithms and multi-metric analyses. Two experimental setups — a local system simulating an edge environment and a scalable FABRIC testbed — were used to evaluate the effectiveness of their algorithms.

## Best Paper

### Running GraphBLAS on FABRIC

*Vaneshi Ramdhony & Hyunsuk Bang*

This paper describes the first FABRIC deployment of GraphBLAS — a powerful linear algebra-based framework for network traffic analysis. The work provides a foundation for further research on network security by deploying an advanced network monitoring technique on FABRIC and making this deployment shareable with other FABRIC users.
