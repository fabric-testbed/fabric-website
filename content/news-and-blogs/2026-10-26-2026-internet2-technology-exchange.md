The 2026 Internet2 Technology Exchange (TechEX26), Oct. 26-30 in Minneapolis, Minn., is the premier annual technical event for the global research and education (R&E) community. This is where chief technologists, engineers, cloud architects, security analysts, IAM professionals, researchers, and more come together to solve real-world challenges.

TechEX26 delivers interactive sessions, hands-on learning, working meetings, and unconference experiences that bridge the strategic and operational. Expect practical takeaways that you can apply immediately, plus meaningful collaboration with peers and industry experts who understand the R&E environment.
