**FABRIC In The News**

_Researchers at Danaides and Clemson University to utilize FABRIC to further develop an app to help civilians in disaster zones_

FABRIC Testbed will assist with verification for P2PR2P, an app that is designed to help victims of conflict organize their own first response. Initially conceived by the French non-governmental organization Danaides, an interdisciplinary group of Clemson faculty and students created the app to securely connect civilians in conflict- and natural-disaster zones with people who can provide food, medicine, transportation and other aid.

Danaides will do its verification work on the FABRIC Testbed, which enables a wide range of computer engineering and network research.

“We look forward to collaborating with Danaides, Dr. Brooks and his students to help them advance P2PR2P,” said K.C. Wang, co-principal investigator on FABRIC. “The FABRIC Testbed is the ideal research infrastructure to meet their needs.”

Read the [full article](https://news.clemson.edu/app-created-to-help-those-in-need-raises-clemson-universitys-international-profile/?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_lefgBo-X_3JSmHOd9YJVpwp4XMcwHpxl0KEVyVS8NCvPryP8noLXxxR4FpwXkA2ocYbCp) online.

**Defining FABRIC**

_A glossary for common terms used by our researchers_

A **hank** is a FABRIC site or a node composed of servers and switches. The term comes from the textile world, where a hank is [a coiled or wrapped unit of yarn or twine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_\(unit_of_measure\)?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_lefgBo-X_3JSmHOd9YJVpwp4XMcwHpxl0KEVyVS8NCvPryP8noLXxxR4FpwXkA2ocYbCp).
