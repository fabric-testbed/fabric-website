*FABRIC used to test federated learning approach across geographically distributed data sites*

Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory are working to use APPFL (Advanced Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning) to enhance climate modeling. The goal is to train large-scale machine-learning climate models without having to move the large amounts of data typically required.

Using FABRIC, the researchers are combining the testbed's network connectivity and distributed compute hardware with APPFL to train models by transferring only the model weights rather than the data itself.

The researchers successfully carried out a demonstration experiment that connected a server node at the University of Michigan with two client nodes in Salt Lake City, training a shallow convolutional neural network using the FedCompass semi-asynchronous algorithm.

"FABRIC offers the capability to understand the role of the network when training models across geographically distributed sites using large amounts of data at each site," said Ravi Madduri, a senior computer scientist at Argonne.
