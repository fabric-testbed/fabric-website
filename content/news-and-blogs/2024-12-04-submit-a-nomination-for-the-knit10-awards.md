FABRIC would like to acknowledge the amazing work of FABRIC users to date. We are excited to announce that we are soliciting nominations for awards that will be presented at KNIT10 next year.

There are four categories that highlight the different ways FABRIC experiments are impacting our communities research. Winners will be selected by evaluating innovative uses of FABRIC resources, experiment design, and execution.

Nominations must actively use FABRIC and “Best FABRIC Experiment” nominations must be packaged as an artifact in the Artifact Manager.

Users can nominate themselves or a colleague for the following awards:

-   Best Published Paper
-   Best FABRIC Matrix
-   Best FABRIC Experiment
-   Best Classroom Use of FABRIC

Award winners will be announced during KNIT10. All nominations must be submitted by February 24, 2025 using our [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeTp3i2iDhB7bHgN8ryMxZci8ya87yjeQd7_JMZImUodNinVA/viewform).
