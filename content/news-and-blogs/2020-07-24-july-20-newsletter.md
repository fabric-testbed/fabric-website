**Round Tables**

Based on the feedback from the Community Workshop, the FABRIC Leadership Team is scheduling a series of round tables designed to foster idea exchange. Read more about the two upcoming Round Tables below.

###### **Hackathon**

_July 27, 2020_

Fabric leadership will discuss ideas and best practices for using students to do pen testing and software black box testing on FABRIC.

###### Industry

_October 2020_

We will convene industry experts in AI, Machine Learning, IoT, Networking and InfoSec to gather their input about the usefulness of FABRIC for future experimentation.

* * *

**2nd FABRIC Workshop: Facility Partners**

_Dec. 2020_

Mark your calendars - the 2nd FABRIC Workshop: Facility Partners Workshop will be held virtually in December 2020. Our target audience includes campus IT, regional, national and international research & education networks, major experimental facilities, and supercomputing centers. Our focus will be on the logistics of federation, connectivity, operation, and user support.

**FABRIC: Creating a Testbed of Testbeds at PEARC20**

_July 31, 2020 at 8 AM PST (11 AM EST)_

Catch members of the FABRIC team at this year's virtual PEARC20 conference. Joined by four additional panelists, hear about plans to develop and experiment on the FABRIC testbed; then, ask questions during the Q&A session. Learn more about the session on [our website](https://fabric-testbed.net/events/pearc20?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8imoGErU30NRamwWzLfwoBLPLtaLPuosa0FRwJj2gwPmJ29CTGa1CNBYLLEmpJjwR7Y2KR).

###### Malathi Veeraraghavan

Dr. Malathi Veeraraghavan, member of the FABRIC team, passed away in May of this year. [Malathi’s career](https://news.virginia.edu/content/memoriam-malathi-veeraraghavan-champion-ideas-and-experiences?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8imoGErU30NRamwWzLfwoBLPLtaLPuosa0FRwJj2gwPmJ29CTGa1CNBYLLEmpJjwR7Y2KR) included many accomplishments and many lives touched. In particular, Malathi made deep contributions to GENI and helped [shape the vision for FABRIC](https://fabric-testbed.net/news/fabric-honors-malathi-veeraraghavan?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8imoGErU30NRamwWzLfwoBLPLtaLPuosa0FRwJj2gwPmJ29CTGa1CNBYLLEmpJjwR7Y2KR); we plan to create a system that would make her proud.
