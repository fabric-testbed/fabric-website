**Supercomputing 2019**

The FABRIC team enjoyed meeting many of you at SC19 in Denver. 50+ people attended FABRIC talks held in booth 781 throughout the conference. We would also like to thank our partners who had booths at the show for sharing information, handouts, and stickers: the University of Kentucky, Clemson University, Georgia Institute of Technology, Internet2, National Center for Supercomputing Applications, Rutgers University, San Diego Supercomputing Center, Texas Advanced Computing Center, and the University of Utah.
