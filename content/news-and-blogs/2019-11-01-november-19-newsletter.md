Through your involvement in similar communities, you may be interested in the new [$20M networking testbed called FABRIC](https://fabric-testbed.net/news/fabric-award?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_daSBUNDGxwKQmnE7NGt6oFu10PEiP1M4bOWTShod1a7xfAWn3kEitZkPhZ47rhm4NRybi).

FABRIC is a unique national research infrastructure to enable cutting-edge and exploratory research at-scale in networking, cybersecurity, distributed computing and storage systems, machine learning, and science applications.

**FABRIC Kick-Off**

![FABRIC-kickoff-group-1](https://hs-6342968.f.hubspotemail.net/hub/6342968/hubfs/FABRIC-kickoff-group-1.jpg?upscale=true&width=1120&upscale=true&name=FABRIC-kickoff-group-1.jpg)

FABRIC collaborators (pictured above) gathered at RENCI in Chapel Hill, NC on Oct. 1 to officially kick off the work of reimagining the future of the Internet.

**Science Design Drivers will be a core part of our work.** **Learn more about each of them below.** 

![GeorgiaTechLogo-black-RGB-TechGold](https://hs-6342968.f.hubspotemail.net/hub/6342968/hubfs/GeorgiaTechLogo-black-RGB-TechGold.png?upscale=true&width=320&upscale=true&name=GeorgiaTechLogo-black-RGB-TechGold.png)

**Russell Clark, GT**

[Smart Sea Level Sensors](https://www.sealevelsensors.org/?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_daSBUNDGxwKQmnE7NGt6oFu10PEiP1M4bOWTShod1a7xfAWn3kEitZkPhZ47rhm4NRybi) is one of the application test beds that will be used in validating FABRIC's effectiveness. Georgia Tech's emphasis will be on resilience and survivability in order to ensure networks remain active during storms.

![sri\_logo\_blue31](https://hs-6342968.f.hubspotemail.net/hub/6342968/hubfs/SDD%20Logos/sri_logo_blue31.jpg?upscale=true&width=320&upscale=true&name=sri_logo_blue31.jpg)

**Phil Porras, SRI**

SRI will focus on leveraging FABRIC’s connection to public clouds to experiment with the design of a Reinforcement Learning Engine that integrates into the networking layer for managing security across clouds.

![uva-logo](https://hs-6342968.f.hubspotemail.net/hub/6342968/hubfs/SDD%20Logos/uva-logo.jpg?upscale=true&width=320&upscale=true&name=uva-logo.jpg)

**Malathi Veeraraghavan, UVA**

UVA will focus on using FABRIC to test their cross-layer network transport and real-time online cyberattack detection at a much larger scale and higher bandwidth than they have previously been able to access.  

![fiuhorizontaltrans](https://hs-6342968.f.hubspotemail.net/hub/6342968/hubfs/fiuhorizontaltrans.png?upscale=true&width=320&upscale=true&name=fiuhorizontaltrans.png)

**Alex Afanasyev, FIU**

FIU will focus on evaluating Named Data Networking (NDN) at scale as a native implementation, rather than an overlay on an IP network.
