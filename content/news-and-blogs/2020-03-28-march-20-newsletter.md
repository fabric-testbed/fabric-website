FABRIC is a unique national research infrastructure to enable cutting-edge and exploratory research at-scale in networking, cybersecurity, distributed computing and storage systems, machine learning, and science applications.

* * *

**COVID-19 Update**

FABRIC leadership has been monitoring the spread of the coronavirus disease (COVID-19) constantly, and **we have decided to change the April 2020 Community Workshop to virtual attendance**. This decision was based on the latest scientific data being reported and the fact that a large number of registered attendees for this meeting would have had to travel. [Click here](https://info.whatisfabric.net/community-workshop-registration?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8yBRuWlRgJUoYYRObC3xT6yJe-EeS8zqAY7wwe4MlfhG4zNS3sUJYg_MzzplOh2faQNWWb) to register for the workshop.

In addition to hosting this virtual workshop, we will be adding a face-to-face workshop to our Fall schedule.

**SC20 Call for Participation**

_Submission Deadline: April 1st_

The Women in IT Networking at SC (WINS) program is now accepting applications for the 2020 program. This program has provided an immersive "hands-on" mentorship opportunity for early-to mid-career women in the IT field who are selected to participate in the ground-up construction of SCinet, which is built annually for the Supercomputing Conference. Learn more about the program by reading the  [PDF announcement](https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/6342968/2020WINS_CFP_Final.pdf?utm_campaign=Newsletters&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8yBRuWlRgJUoYYRObC3xT6yJe-EeS8zqAY7wwe4MlfhG4zNS3sUJYg_MzzplOh2faQNWWb).
