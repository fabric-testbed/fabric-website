Reasons why your project might be denoting that review is required

-   Project has expired
-   Additional information is required
-   Irregular use of the testbed

**Project has expired**

Upon creation a project is given a one year expiry date. About one month prior to the project expiring a notice will be displayed on the project landing page with instructions on how to extend the project. If the project is not extended it will reach its expiry date at which point it will be rendered non-active. Non-active projects are no longer able to deploy into the testbed and marked as requiring review prior to becoming active again.

This is also a good time to review the resources that have been allocated to your project and determine if they are still relevant for your needs.

**Additional information is required**

FABRIC is an NSF sponsored research testbed. Projects are initially given a general name and description, but over time it is expected that researchers will update their project metadata to help the FABRIC team and the NSF better understand your project's impact on the community. Examples of information that may be requested are publication references that cite FABRIC, funding information, community keywords, project topics, FABRIC matrix entry (website, blog posts, or slide deck that further describes your project), etc.

**Irregular use of the testbed**

This can be anything that goes against the acceptable use agreement, disrupts the approved use of the testbed by others, or is malicious in nature will cause a project to be suspended pending further review.
