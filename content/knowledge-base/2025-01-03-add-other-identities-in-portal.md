On the **My Profile** page, under the **Other Identities** section, users can add their **Google Scholar ID**, **ORCID ID**, or other identity links.

## How to find your Google Scholar ID?

1\. **Log in to Google Scholar**:  
Go to [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) and sign in with your Google account.

2\. **Access Your Profile**:  
If you have already created a profile, click on your profile picture or name in the top right corner to open your profile.

3\. **Locate Your Google Scholar ID**:  
Your Google Scholar ID is the unique string of letters and numbers in the URL of your profile. For example:

```
https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ABCDEFGHIJKL
```

In this URL, `ABCDEFGHIJKL` is your Google Scholar ID.

4\. **Copy the ID**:  
Copy the ID from the URL and paste it to Portal.

## How to find your ORCID ID?

1\. **Log in to ORCID**:  
Visit the [ORCID website](https://orcid.org) and sign in with your credentials.

2\. **Access Your Profile**:  
After logging in, you will be redirected to your ORCID record (profile) page.

3\. **Locate Your ORCID ID**:  
Your ORCID ID is displayed prominently below your name on your profile. It is a unique 16-digit identifier formatted like this:

```
https://orcid.org/0000-0001-2345-6789
```

The 16-digit number (e.g., `0000-0001-2345-6789`) is your ORCID ID.

4\. **Copy Your ORCID ID**:  
You can copy the entire link or just the 16-digit number and paste it to Portal.
