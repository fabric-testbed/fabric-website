## Jupyter Examples and Videos

-   **Hello, FABRIC** ([Jupyter](https://github.com/fabric-testbed/jupyter-examples/blob/master/fabric_examples/fablib_api/hello_fabric/hello_fabric.ipynb), [YouTube](https://youtu.be/eU6FmPb-Hnc)): Submit your first slice!
