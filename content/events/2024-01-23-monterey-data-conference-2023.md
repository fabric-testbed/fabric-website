**Date**: August 21-23, 2023

**Location**: Monterey, CA

**Event Website**: [https://www.montereydataconference.org/2023](https://www.montereydataconference.org/2023)

**Presenter**: Inder Monga

## Event Details

The Monterey Data Conference is an annual invitation-only meeting in Monterey which brings together researchers from DOE national laboratories, facilities, universities, and industry to showcase and discuss the latest advances and open challenges in scientific data analysis and computing.

FABRIC leadership team member Inder Monga will present "FABRIC: A Nation-Wide Infrastructure for Large-Scale Network Experimentation" at 11 AM PT on August 22.
