**Date**: February 19 — 21, 2025

**Location**: Arlington, VA

**Event Website**: [https://events.internet2.edu/website/79982/program/](https://events.internet2.edu/website/79982/program/)

**Presenter**: Anita Nikolich

On behalf of the National Science Foundation and contributing partners, the NAIRR team is excited to invite you to the inaugural NAIRR Pilot Annual Meeting—a gathering of researchers, educators, innovators, and partners of the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot.

**The event is February 19-21 at the Hyatt Regency Crystal City in Arlington, Virginia**. It will highlight resource offerings, AI, science, education and innovation outcomes, and the NAIRR pilot’s progress in democratizing access to AI resources, and its vision for the future of AI research in the United States.
