Date: March 18, 2025

Time: 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM ET

Join us on March 18, 2025, from 3-4 PM ET for *Stitching Together Innovation with FABRIC Users.* This webinar will explore how FABRIC empowers researchers in federated digital pathology, providing a flexible and scalable platform for experiments.

Presenters Cody Bumgardner, Vaiden Logan, and Mitchell Klusty will lead a 30-minute tutorial for new users, including graduate students. They will provide an overview of their experiment, discussing why FABRIC was the ideal choice and highlighting its unique features--seamless data transfer, high-performance computing, and adaptable infrastructure.

A live demo will showcase a distributed systems application that securely transfers medical data between agents and isolated protected networks, ensuring compliance with security constraints. The broader system enables remote data to be accessed as if it were local, creating a unified global namespace for seamless research collaboration.

The webinar will conclude with a 15-minute Q&A session, allowing attendees to engage with the presenters and explore how FABRIC can support their research. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with the FABRIC community and discover new possibilities for your work!
