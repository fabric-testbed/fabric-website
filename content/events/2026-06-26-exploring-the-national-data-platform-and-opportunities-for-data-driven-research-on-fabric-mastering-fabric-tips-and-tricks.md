![](https://learn.fabric-testbed.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/NDP-1024x576.png)

Join us on **July 21, 2026, from 3:00–4:30 PM ET** for a FABRIC Tips & Tricks webinar featuring Dr. Manish Parashar, Co-PI of the National Data Platform (NDP). As data-intensive research continues to grow across scientific disciplines, researchers need robust platforms that can help them discover, access, integrate, and analyze data at scale. In this session, Dr. Parashar will provide an overview of the NDP and its vision for advancing data-driven discovery through a national ecosystem of data services and infrastructure.

Participants will learn how the National Data Platform supports researchers by connecting data resources, computational capabilities, and advanced services to enable more effective scientific workflows. Dr. Parashar will discuss the platform's architecture, current capabilities, and its role within the broader national research infrastructure landscape. The webinar will also explore potential opportunities for leveraging FABRIC's programmable experimentation environment alongside NDP services to support future data-centric research and innovation.

Whether you are interested in large-scale data analysis, cyberinfrastructure, or emerging opportunities at the intersection of networking, computing, and data science, this session will provide valuable insights into how national research platforms can work together to accelerate scientific discovery.
