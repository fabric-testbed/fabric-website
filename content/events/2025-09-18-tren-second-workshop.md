**FABRIC Presentation at the Tennessee Research and Education Network (TREN) Workshop**  
At the TREN Workshop, **Paul presented “FABRIC: Experimental Research and Educational Networks,”** highlighting how FABRIC supports innovation in networking research and education. The **Tennessee Research and Education Network (TREN)**—funded by the **National Science Foundation** and led by four under-resourced public universities—is building a **statewide 100G research backbone** to connect Tennessee’s institutions, enhance research capacity, and foster collaboration across academia, government, and non-profit sectors.

Paul Ruth presented at the event.
