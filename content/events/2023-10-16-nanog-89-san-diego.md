**Date**: October 16-18, 2023

**Location**: San Diego, CA

**Event Website**: [https://www.nanog.org/events/nanog-89/](https://www.nanog.org/events/nanog-89/)

**Presenter**: Anita Nikolich

## Event Details

NANOG is, and always has been, dedicated to the people who make up the community. The tri-annual meetings draw up to 1,500 individuals in multiple facets of network engineering, operations, and architecture, who gather in major cities across North America. NANOG 89 will take place October 16 - 18, 2023 in San Diego, CA

FABRIC leadership team member Anita Nikolich will present a lightning talk on FABIRC on October 18. The FABRIC testbed is designed to help redefine the boundaries of what's possible in networking, security and distributed systems. Nikolich will do a quick overview of its capabilities and tell attendees about how they can use this new, freely available testbed.
