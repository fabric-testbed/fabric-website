**FABRIC Participation at the 2025 NSF Cybersecurity Summit**  
A representative from **FABRIC** attended the 2025 NSF Cybersecurity Summit to contribute to two sessions that highlight emerging cybersecurity priorities. At the **BoF on “PQC Risk Assessments for Scientific Infrastructure,”** they will discuss the use of the **PQSee tool**, which helps assess post-quantum readiness across research testbeds, such as FABRIC. They also **participated in a panel on AI Security**, exploring evolving risks and strategies to safeguard AI systems in scientific environments.

Anita Niolich presented at the event.
